### 2026-10-24 — Compose: product_catalog container exited (code 1) right after `docker compose up`

ERROR (symptom): `docker-product_catalog-1  Exited (1)`; no logs (the app sends logs to the OTel collector); `verify_data.py` then failed with `StatusCode.UNAVAILABLE ... 127.0.0.1:3550 Connection actively refused`.

## Evidence (facts gathered, in order)
1. `docker ps` showed only `docker-db-1` up; `docker ps -a` showed the app `Exited (1)`.
2. `docker inspect` of the app container showed the environment it received: `DB_CONNECTION_STRING=postgres://astronomy-user:***@db:5432/astronomy_db`.
3. `init.sql` line 8 creates the role `astronomy_user` (underscore). The string had a hyphen. **Problem 1: typo.**
4. After fixing the user name, the app still exited. The db log had no `FATAL` / `password authentication failed` / `role does not exist` lines, so the app never got a failed login.
5. The db log showed a fresh initialisation (`initdb`) and "ready to accept connections" a few seconds after start.
6. With the db already ready, `docker start docker-product_catalog-1` worked and `verify_data.py` returned 10 products, with no config change.

## My hypothesis (NOT yet proven)
The app started at the same moment as a fresh Postgres, tried to connect before it was ready, and exited. **Problem 2: startup race.**
